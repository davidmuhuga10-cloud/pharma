Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version:

  • Pharmacies use Pharma to keep records about their customers and staff. The pharmacy decides what is recorded and who can see it. We look after the records for the pharmacy.
  • We use the information only to run Pharma for the pharmacy. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We share it only with the companies that host Pharma, deliver its SMS and process its SMS Wallet top-ups, and with authorities when the law requires it.
  • If you are a pharmacy customer or staff member and want to see, correct or delete your information, start with the pharmacy you dealt with. We will help the pharmacy do it.

1. Who we are

1.1Pharma is stock and sales software made for Kenyan pharmacies, provided by Flovo Digital. "Pharma", "we", "us" and "our" in this policy mean Flovo Digital. You can reach us by email at info@flovodigital.com or on WhatsApp at +254 705 041 512.

1.2This policy explains what personal information Pharma holds, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. It applies to the Pharma web app at pharmamanagement.site and our public pages.

2. Pharmacies are in charge of their records

2.1Most of the information in Pharma is put there by a pharmacy: its customers, staff, sales, stock, credit and claim records. For that information the pharmacy is the data controller. It decides what to record, why, and who can see it. Pharma is the data processor: we store and process the information on the pharmacy's behalf and on its instructions, under the data processing terms in our Terms of Service.

2.2We are the data controller for a smaller set of information: the details of the person who signs a pharmacy up and pays for Pharma, our billing records, the messages we send to pharmacies ourselves, technical information we need to keep Pharma secure, and information about visitors to our public pages.

2.3If you are a pharmacy customer or staff member, the pharmacy you dealt with is usually the right first contact for questions about your information. You can also contact us, and we will pass your request to the pharmacy and help it respond.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • provide Pharma's features to the pharmacy: stock, sales, receipts, suppliers, expenses, customer credit, insurance claims, controlled-drug records and stock takes;
  • deliver the SMS a pharmacy chooses to send, such as refill reminders, credit reminders and custom messages, and send automatic SMS that are part of the service, such as one-time codes;
  • confirm identities when people sign up or reset a password;
  • provide support, fix problems and answer questions;
  • keep Pharma secure, detect misuse, and keep each pharmacy's information separate from every other pharmacy's;
  • bill pharmacies, process SMS Wallet top-ups and keep our own accounts;
  • understand how Pharma is used, using combined and anonymised statistics, so we can improve it; and
  • meet our legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

4.1We do not sell personal information, rent it out, or use it for advertising.

5. Our legal grounds

5.1Where we process information for a pharmacy, we do so on the pharmacy's instructions and under our contract with it. The pharmacy relies on its own lawful grounds, such as running its business, its legal duties and, where needed, consent.

5.2Where we are the controller, we rely on: our contract with the pharmacy (to provide and bill for Pharma); our legitimate interests in keeping Pharma secure, supporting pharmacies and improving the service, which we balance against your rights; and our legal obligations, for example for tax records.

6. Who can see information

6.1Within the pharmacy. Each person sees what their role allows, as set by the pharmacy's owners.

6.2Our service providers. We use a small number of trusted technology companies to run Pharma. They act on our instructions, under contracts that require them to protect the information:

  • Cloud database provider: database, sign-in and secure storage. This is where Pharma's records are kept.
  • Website hosting provider: hosts the Pharma website and app and runs some of its server functions.
  • SMS delivery provider: delivers SMS to phone numbers. It receives the phone number and the text of each message.
  • Mobile money provider: processes SMS Wallet top-up payments.

6.3Our staff. Only authorised staff can access pharmacy records, and only when needed to support a pharmacy, investigate a problem or keep Pharma secure. This kind of access is recorded in an internal audit log and is not shown to the pharmacy's other users. Our staff must keep the information confidential.

6.4Authorities. We disclose information to the police, courts, regulators or other authorities only when the law requires it or a valid legal order compels it. Unless the law forbids it, we tell the pharmacy first.

6.5If Pharma changes hands. If our business is transferred to another owner, pharmacy records would move with it under the same protections, and we would tell pharmacies in advance.

7. How we keep information safe

  • Each pharmacy's records are separated at the database level, so one pharmacy's login can never read another pharmacy's records.
  • Access by role limits what each person can see and change inside a pharmacy.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form, and password resets are confirmed with a one-time code sent to the account's phone number.
  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), and stored data is encrypted by our database provider.
  • Support access is logged, and our own administrative actions are recorded in an audit log.

7.1No system is completely secure. If a breach affects personal information, we will tell the affected pharmacies without delay, help them respond, and notify the authorities where the law requires.

8. How long we keep information

8.1We keep a pharmacy's records for as long as its account is active, so the pharmacy can keep using its history.

8.2Within an active account, the pharmacy decides what to keep, and can delete particular customer or staff records itself.

8.3If a pharmacy asks us to close its account, we delete the account and its records within 30 days of confirming the request. If an account is restricted for unpaid fees, we keep its records for 90 days and then delete them after a final notice.

8.4Copies in our providers' routine backups are overwritten in the normal course of their backup cycles. We keep billing records for as long as tax law requires. Anonymised statistics, which identify no one, may be kept longer.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be told how your information is used (this policy does that);
  • see the information held about you;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong or misleading;
  • have it deleted where it is no longer needed or is held unlawfully;
  • object to some uses of it, or ask for its use to be restricted; and
  • receive a copy in a common format, where this applies.

9.1Because the pharmacy controls its records, please make your request to the pharmacy first. You can also write to us, and we will check your identity, work with the pharmacy, and respond within the time the law allows.

9.2If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, please tell us so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

10. Cookies and storage on your device

10.1When you sign in, Pharma stores a sign-in token in your browser so that you stay signed in, and saves a small amount of your own account data (such as your profile and recent dashboard figures) so the app loads faster and some screens remain viewable without a connection. Signing out removes your sign-in token. These are needed for Pharma to work, and we do not use them to track you.

10.2Pharma and our public pages do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. See our Cookie Policy for the full list of what is stored and why.

10.3On a shared computer, always sign out when you finish.

11. Messages from us

11.1We send pharmacy owners messages about their account, such as security notices, one-time codes, billing reminders and important changes to Pharma. These are part of the service.

11.2We may also send owners occasional news about Pharma features. You can stop these at any time by emailing us or messaging us on WhatsApp. SMS a pharmacy sends to its own customers (refill reminders, credit reminders, custom messages) are the pharmacy's own communications, sent on its instructions; a customer who does not want them can be marked "do not text" in Pharma by the pharmacy.

12. Changes to this policy

12.1We update this policy when Pharma or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how we use personal information, we will tell pharmacy owners before it takes effect.
