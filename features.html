Everything your pharmacy needs to know its stock

Pharma tracks stock by batch and expiry date, records every sale, and keeps the records you need for suppliers, expenses, customer credit, insurance claims and controlled drugs — on any phone.

What it does

Batch and expiry tracking

Stock is tracked by batch and expiry date, so the oldest stock sells first and you get a warning before anything expires.

Sales and receipts

Record every sale with split payments — cash, M-Pesa, credit, insurance or bank — and print or share the receipt.

Restocking and suppliers

Receive stock from your suppliers, keep their records, and manage purchase orders and returns.

Expenses

Record running costs next to your sales, so you see real profit, not just revenue.

Insurance claims

Track claims from submitted through to paid or rejected, alongside your cash and M-Pesa sales.

Customer credit

Keep a running balance of what each customer owes you, with a credit limit if you want one.

Controlled-drug records

Keep the records you choose to keep for controlled drugs, alongside whatever your own licence requires.

Stock take

Count your stock and record the differences you find against what the system shows.

Cash drawer

Open and close your till with a recorded float, so cash sales reconcile at the end of the day.

SMS reminders

Send refill reminders, credit reminders and custom messages from a prepaid wallet you top up by M-Pesa, plus a daily evening summary to the owner.

Reports

Daily, weekly and custom-range reports on sales, stock value and profit, exportable to Excel.

Install from your browser

Installs as an app on any phone or computer straight from pharmamanagement.site — no app store needed.

Questions people ask

Does Pharma track expiry dates?

Yes. Stock is tracked by batch and expiry date, so the oldest sells first, and you're warned before anything expires.

Can I record customer credit and insurance claims?

Yes. Pharma keeps customer credit balances and tracks insurance claims in the same system as your other sales.

Does it keep controlled-drug records?

Yes, as a record-keeping tool. Your pharmacy remains responsible for its own regulatory obligations.

Can I send reminder SMS?

Yes — refill and credit reminders, custom messages, and an evening summary, paid from a wallet you top up by M-Pesa.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. New pharmacies get a free trial — see our pricing page for how it works after that.
