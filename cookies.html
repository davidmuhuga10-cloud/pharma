Cookie Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version:

  • Pharma does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not run any third-party analytics.
  • Everything Pharma stores in your browser is there to make the app work: keeping you signed in, loading faster, and letting a few screens work briefly offline.
  • Pharma has no cookie banner because it has nothing to opt in to — there is nothing here beyond what the app needs to run.

1. What this page covers

1.1This page explains what Pharma stores in your browser or device, and why. "Pharma", "we" and "us" mean Flovo Digital, the provider of Pharma. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1.2Pharma does not set traditional tracking cookies at all. Instead, like most modern web apps, it uses your browser's own local storage and a small offline cache. The table below lists everything it stores. This page, and the way we handle any related personal data, follows Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, and the laws of Kenya generally.

2. What Pharma stores and why

WhatPurposeCleared when
Sign-in sessionKeeps you signed in between visits, so you don't have to log in every time you open Pharma.You sign out, or it expires
Profile and pharmacy cacheRemembers your own profile and pharmacy so the app can draw its screens instantly while it reloads the latest data.You sign out
Dashboard snapshotKeeps your last-seen sales figures so the dashboard shows real numbers immediately instead of a blank loading screen.You sign out, or you open a different date range
Sign-up in progressIf you're part-way through creating a pharmacy account, remembers where you left off.Sign-up finishes, or you clear it

Offline app filesA copy of the app's own code and loading screen, kept by your browser's service worker, so Pharma still opens (though it needs a connection to load or save your data) if your internet drops briefly.We publish an update, or you clear site data
Light theme preferenceIf you've chosen a display preference in the app, remembers it for next time.You change it, or clear site data

2.1All of this lives only in your own browser, on your own device. None of it is shared between pharmacies, used to track you across other websites, or sold to anyone. It is not read by us unless you contact us for support and we need to help you debug something on your own device.

3. What Pharma does not use

  • No advertising cookies, and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or behaviour-tracking scripts.
  • No social-media tracking pixels.
  • No cross-site tracking of any kind.

3.1If this ever changes, we will update this page first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before anything new is set.

4. Clearing what's stored

4.1Signing out of Pharma clears your sign-in session and your personal caches. To remove everything, including the offline app files, use your browser's "Clear site data" or "Clear browsing data" option for pharmamanagement.site. Doing this will sign you out and Pharma will need a connection to reload.

4.2On a shared or public computer, always sign out when you finish using Pharma.

5. Changes to this policy

5.1We update this page when what Pharma stores changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
